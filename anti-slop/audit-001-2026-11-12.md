## Findings

1. **HIGH, R-02 em dash in UI text.** `src/pages/index.astro:34` (masthead line "W4NN4D13 — SECURITY RESEARCHER") and `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro:219` (footer "W4NN4D13 — SECURITY RESEARCHER"). Both are agent-written UI text. Fix: replace the em dash with a comma, colon, or parentheses.

2. **HIGH, R-25 contrast.** `--fg-subtle: #888888` on `--bg: #ffffff` gives about 3.5:1, below the 4.5:1 minimum for small text. It is used for the masthead eyebrow (`index.astro:33`), sidebar category counts (`index.astro:155`), and hashtag counts (`index.astro:172`). Fix: darken `--fg-subtle` to at least 4.5:1 on `--bg`, or use `--fg-muted` (#555, about 7.5:1) for small text.

3. **MEDIUM, R-06 typography.** `--font-ui` and `--font-mono` are both `'Courier New'`. The H1 ("w4nn4d13"), featured titles, and section heads use it, and most labels are uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`global.css` around lines 94–97, 238–242, 259–263). No written reason for the choice exists. Fix: pick a typeface for a stated brand reason, or record why the monospace voice is the identity (R-31).

4. **MEDIUM, eyebrow above the H1.** `index.astro:33–37`: the small uppercase line "W4NN4D13 — SECURITY RESEARCHER · AI ENGINEER" sits above the H1 "w4nn4d13", which repeats the name. Fix: cut the duplicate, or fold the role into the subheadline (Part 1, Eyebrow Badge pattern; R-31).

5. **MEDIUM, R-14 identical feature cards.** The three featured posts (`index.astro:53–68`) share the same bordered box, label, title, description, and meta row, with no hierarchy reason. Fix: give the lead post a different treatment than the two supporting posts, and write the reason down.

6. **MEDIUM, R-09 badge combination.** `index.astro:123–124` stacks a filled "FEATURED" badge and a bordered category badge on each row. The tag badges in the featured cards (`index.astro:63–65`) are also uppercase bordered capsules. Fix: keep one status marker per row, and only where it carries real state.

7. **LOW, R-08 arrows.** Arrows on "All articles →" (`index.astro:51`), "All →" (`:103`), "View all 22 articles →" (`:133`), and "Browse 22 articles by date →" (`:187`). Fix: remove them, or keep one where the direction matters.

8. **LOW, content-owned, R-37 (owner decision, not auto-fixed).** The "The Forgotten Access" featured description contains 💀 and 😭. The description is post content, not agent copy. Question for owner: keep the emoji or drop it?

9. **LOW, content-owned, R-37 (owner decision, not auto-fixed).** The post title "CVE-2026-32138 — API Key Exposure in Nexulean Website" contains an em dash (R-02). The title comes from the post's frontmatter, so it is user writing. Question for owner: keep the em dash or change it?
